# Preserve scope and depth

**Basis:** User requirements, not a proven cross-model mitigation.

Remove content that does not help fulfil the request.
Preserve details that affect correctness, implementation or a real decision.
Measure task success and omissions, not only word count.

Related observation: [ChatGPT clutter](../observations/chatgpt-clutter.md).
Behavioural effectiveness remains subject to the declared evaluation cases.
